About Me

During my time at SAIT I’ve found that each semester grows upon the last. We started with a residential project which helped grow a love of room design, and flow of spaces. This helped in the second semester as we worked on a residential, commercial mixed- use building. This helped me to begin to understand the requirements of commercial spaces, which grew in the third semester as we worked on a commercial office building. I’ve worked with wood framing and steel and looking forward to this semester I hope to learn concrete construction giving me a broad scope of different construction methods and details to refer to in my future career. I look forward to working on this hotel project as it will allow me to spend more time working on a different but similar style of room design to residential and look closer at other mechanical systems such as those required by a pool or a restaurant located within a hotel. I hope to round out my experience and strive to create a design which will bring beauty and functionality to the communities my designs are located in.
